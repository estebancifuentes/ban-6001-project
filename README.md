# Predicting Online News Popularity

Statistics and regression analysis exploring what predicts how many times an online news article gets shared, using the UCI Online News Popularity dataset.

## Dataset

- **Source:** UCI Machine Learning Repository — [Online News Popularity Data Set](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/332/online+news+popularity), based on Fernandes, Vinagre & Cortez (2015), *"A Proactive Intelligent Decision Support System for Predicting the Popularity of Online News."*
- **Topic:** Characteristics of online news articles (word counts, links, images, sentiment, publish timing, category) and their relationship to social media shares.
- **Size:** 39,644 articles; 17 continuous variables and 3 categorical variables selected from the original 60 columns.
- **Main research question:** Can article-level features (content, structure, sentiment, category, timing) predict or explain how many times an article is shared?
